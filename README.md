## About Me

I'm an Electronics and Communication Engineering student with a strong interest in embedded systems, microcontrollers, electronics troubleshooting, and firmware development.

I enjoy understanding how electronic hardware works, writing firmware that interacts with the physical world, and integrating individual components into functional systems. My focus is on learning through implementation — building projects, debugging hardware, testing system behavior, and improving designs through practical experimentation.

My engineering interests include:

- **Embedded Systems:** Microcontroller programming, peripheral interfacing, and firmware logic.
- **Electronics:** Circuit fundamentals, sensor integration, signal measurement, and hardware debugging.
- **Connected Devices:** ESP32-based systems, wireless communication, and IoT monitoring.
- **System Development:** Combining hardware, firmware, communication, and user interfaces into a coherent solution.

I aim to develop reliable, well-documented embedded solutions while strengthening my understanding of electronics, low-level programming, and system-level design.

## Engineering Approach

I believe engineering is more than making a project work once. It involves understanding the problem, choosing suitable components, designing the logic, testing real behavior, identifying limitations, and documenting the result.

My approach is to:

1. Understand the problem and define the expected system behavior.
2. Select appropriate microcontrollers, sensors, and electronic components.
3. Develop firmware with clear logic and modular structure.
4. Integrate hardware and software, then debug unexpected behavior.
5. Test the system under different conditions and document improvements.

I am particularly interested in projects that connect electronics with practical applications rather than treating hardware and software as separate disciplines.
