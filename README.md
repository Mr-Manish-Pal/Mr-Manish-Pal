## ⚡ About Me

I am an **Embedded Systems & IoT Engineer in the making**, currently pursuing my **B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering**. I enjoy working at the intersection of electronics, firmware, and connected systems — turning circuit-level ideas into functional hardware prototypes.

My interests extend beyond writing code. I focus on understanding how microcontrollers interact with sensors, how firmware controls physical hardware, and how embedded devices communicate with other systems.

- 🚀 **Current Focus:** Exploring FreeRTOS, dual-core ESP32 programming, embedded networking, and power optimization.
- 🛠️ **Hardware Experience:** Hands-on work with microcontrollers, sensor interfacing, circuit debugging, and embedded prototypes.
- 🌐 **IoT Development:** Building sensor-driven systems with connectivity, monitoring, and dashboard integration.
- 🔬 **Engineering Approach:** Hardware-first experimentation, modular firmware, practical debugging, and iterative testing.
- 🎬 **Content Creator:** Sharing engineering knowledge through my YouTube channel, **Code Mode On**.
- 🎯 **Career Goal:** To grow as an Embedded Systems Engineer and develop reliable, practical hardware-software solutions.

## 🔬 How I Approach Engineering

I believe embedded engineering is more than making a circuit work once. A useful prototype should have understandable firmware, documented hardware connections, predictable behavior, and testable requirements.

My project workflow focuses on:

- **Understand:** Identify the system requirements and hardware constraints.
- **Integrate:** Connect sensors, microcontrollers, and output devices.
- **Develop:** Implement firmware and organize the control logic.
- **Debug:** Investigate hardware and software issues systematically.
- **Validate:** Test actual behavior against expected results.
- **Document:** Make the design easier to understand, reproduce, and extend.

My long-term goal is to apply these fundamentals to robust embedded products, connected devices, and real-world automation systems.
